Create Lineup feature icons with simple planar symbols and the cheerful palette approved
by Henrique on 2026-10-14.
Use the selected versioned reference named in Design/FeatureIcons/manifest.json as the visual
authority. Match its clean luminous palette, bold crisp shapes, gently rounded motif geometry
and modest gradients. Give each tool one cheerful principal hue and a warm white or off-white symbol.
Use related lighter/darker tones only when they improve meaning or contrast.

Choose fresh app-icon colors: juicy tangerine, sunny yellow, spring mint, clear turquoise,
rose, blue and violet. Match the selected family sources and manifest palette. Color should
feel bright and happy at small sizes. Earlier dusty terracotta, brown, ochre, olive and grayish
palettes were rejected. Keep a single color family per icon; brightness comes from a clean
background hue, not multicolor symbols or stronger lighting.

Keep one recognizable feature concept, centered and visually balanced. Use a few substantial
shapes, thick strokes and generous gaps that remain clear at 16 and 20 px. A typical motif
covers about 60-66% of the canvas in its larger dimension; preserve approved existing geometry
when editing. Palette and composition should be interesting through the symbol and proportions,
without complementary color accents, rainbow glyphs, neon or miniature decoration.

Center symmetric subjects on the canvas axes. Check whole-glyph bounds and the central shape
after native preparation; balanced internal margins and optical weight matter at small sizes.
Preserve approved simple concepts when refining them. World Clock uses its single split dial;
Display Control uses its simple monitor outline. Menu Bar integrates horizontal collapse
into one app bar rather than adding a detached dropdown arrow.

Gradients are gentle planar color transitions. Keep surfaces graphic, with crisp edges.
Exclude sculpted relief, bevels, glossy rims, radial lighting, inflated tile volume and shadows.

Generate exactly one standalone fully opaque square PNG of at least 1024 px per call.
The background extends continuously to all four edges and all four corners. Supply no outer
margin, padding, tile silhouette or baked outer corner mask. The shared native preparation
step applies the macOS corner shape and exports edge-to-edge feature masters. Internal motif
spacing is intentional composition; it must not become transparent padding around the tile.
Feature preparation uses the native renderer's alpha shape only, with the source artwork's
RGB. The export must not add lighting, white edge bands or border strokes. Inspect actual
outer-edge pixels and the running Settings header before accepting a prepared family.

Preserve original generated bytes, exact prompts, references, tool arguments and SHA-256.
Do not infer a model version from the name of a tool. The integrated imagegen service does not
expose its model selector/version. Keep previous approved sources and references attributable.

Feature manifests use style "restrained-v1", framing "edge-to-edge" and logical size 72.
All features export at exactly 72/144/216 px and share the 20 pt sidebar / 56 pt header renderer.
Validate real alpha, continuous opaque edge bands, metadata and small-size appearance before
integration. Correct inconsistent artwork; keep UI scales uniform across the family.

The application logo retains its existing three-pane symbol and has a separate native macOS
Icon Composer workflow. Its platform mask and spacing are owned by Apple's app-icon contract,
not the zero-padding feature-image contract. Keep the menu-bar mark monochrome.
