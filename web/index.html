Draw your own window zones on macOS.

Draw the exact zones you work in, then drop any window into one with a drag or a keyboard shortcut.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
  • Free, forever
  • Open source (MIT)
  • No tracking, no account
  • Native and lightweight
  • macOS 13+

Design your own zones

Two columns, three columns, a big main area with a stack beside it. Split a zone as many times as you like until it fits the way you work.

Every screen remembers

Your laptop and your desk monitor can be set up differently. Lineup switches between them on its own as you move windows around.

Quick to place

Hold Shift and drag a window onto a zone, or use a keyboard shortcut. Windows land where you want them, every time.

Up and running in a minute

  1. Download the app and drag it into your Applications folder.
  2. Allow it once in System Settings, then turn on Accessibility so Lineup can move your windows.
  3. Edit your layout from the menu bar and start snapping.
