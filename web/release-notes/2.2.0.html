Lineup 2.2.0

Adds Keep Awake, an optional tool for preventing idle sleep during a timed session. Enable it in Settings, then start a 15-minute, 30-minute, 1-hour or 2-hour session from Settings or the menu bar. See the remaining time and stop whenever you want.

Keeping the display on is a separate option, off by default. Changing that option preserves the remaining time. Sessions end on expiry, disable, quit or explicit sleep, and never resume automatically after wake or relaunch.

Zones can also restore apps to their last used zone when they open. Enable this option in Zones settings to remember placements made with Lineup.
